I taught myself to code making Unity games back in 2018, drifted into full-stack, and these days I’m mostly on .NET, AI and embedded, taking things from the firmware up to the cloud as CTO at Campfire Toys.

I work remotely with teams around the world, and I enjoy solving complex problems, shaping product architecture, and shipping real, usable systems.

### 💼 Now

- **CTO & Product Strategy Lead** at [Campfire Toys, Inc.](https://campfiremusic.ai) · since Mar 2025
- **Technical Consultant** at [Arkade, Inc.](https://arkade.games) · since Dec 2025
- Previously **HQ Engineering & HCB Operations** at [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) · Jun 2023 – Feb 2025

### 🧪 What I’m focused on

- Designing **AI-driven interactive devices**
- Defining **product architecture** across firmware, backend and cloud
- Building scalable APIs and backend services
- Coordinating with manufacturers on **production-ready hardware**
- Iterating after launch based on real user feedback
